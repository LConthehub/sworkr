Money & houses

Workplaces & platforms

Everywhere you earn: a club, a house, an agency, a fan site, a phone line. Add them under Money → Income & expenses → Workplaces & platforms, or tick your kinds of work and Sworkr adds the usual ones. Each has its own rules:

  • Their share of what you earn, a fee per shift, a share of gifts, a share of tips.
  • Deductions: any number, named however the workplace names them. Each is a percentage or an amount, taken off the top (before the house's share) or out of your share, and can be set to apply always.
  • Paid out later: income there starts as "not paid out yet" until the workplace pays you.
  • Charges you pay them: room and board, a house doctor, room hire. Tours at a workplace log these for you.

When you add income, the form works out the fees from those rules and shows what you keep. Tick the deductions that applied; they come off in the order the workplace takes them.

Pay you haven't received yet

On Income & expenses, Not paid out to you yet shows what each workplace owes you. When they pay, press Paid out and say how: it marks everything received.

How it was paid

Every income entry can say how it was paid: cash, bank transfer, check, a payment app. It shows in the list and in your records.

Tips

Add a tip on a booking (the Tip field) or from the + $ button on any page (Money in / Tip / Money out). If the workplace keeps a share of tips, it's worked out and taken off.

Rates & deposits

Money → Rates is your rate card. Pick a service when you book and its length and price fill in. Copy as text gives you something to send. Deposits to collect lists bookings ahead whose deposit hasn't come in; On an old rate lists clients whose last booking cost less than your card says now.

Tours

A tour (Calendar → + New → Tour) gets its own tour statement: income, fees, costs, health tests, and what you kept. Its tour plan holds a pack list (from your own templates), a budget against what you actually spend, and meals & snacks whose groceries go onto the pack list. Money → Compare → By tour sets tours side by side, per working day.

Comparing

Money → Compare: by tour, month, week, kind of work, workplace, persona, how they found you (income against ad spend) and busy times (a grid of when bookings happen, and what an hour of each kind brings in).

Taxes

Money → Taxes adds up the year: income by kind, fees, expenses by category, your profit, mileage and estimated payments, with quarterly due dates and reminders. Download as CSV gives your accountant a spreadsheet; Print or save as PDF gives a page. Sworkr adds up your records. It isn't tax advice.

A license or work card is paid to the government, not to a workplace: keep it under Settings → My work → Licenses & documents, and when you renew it press Renewed to move the date and log what you paid under Fees & licenses.
