Clients & inquiries

Contacts

Contacts → Everyone holds clients, collaborators (another worker, a photographer, a driver: anyone you work with) and others. A name is optional, so a handle alone is fine. Each contact can have handles, a persona (the name they know you by), preferences, a birthday, screening status and references.

  • Screening: unscreened, pending, verified or declined, with notes on what you checked.
  • Do not see: tick it and Sworkr warns you if you try to book that person. A private bad-date list can be imported, or downloaded for your own backup, under Settings → Security & privacy.
  • How they found you: an ad site, an account, a referral. This is what makes Money → Compare → By how they found you work: income against what you spent on each.
  • Tags group people, and can change their price (below).

Inquiries

Contacts → Inquiries tracks everyone who got in touch: new, replied, screening, deposit, booked, or lost (with why). Move each along as it happens. Below the list: how far inquiries get, where they drop out, and which sources send people who actually book. Make a contact turns an inquiry into a contact when they're worth keeping. Your saved text is one tap away for replies, and can fill in {name}, {day}, {time} and {wanted} from the inquiry.

Ghosts

Contacts → Ghosts lists regulars you've seen at least twice who haven't booked for a while and have nothing booked ahead, biggest spenders first, with how often they used to book.

Prices by tag

Money → Rates → Prices by tag: give a tag a percentage off, an amount off, or a locked-in rate (the client keeps paying what they last paid for that service). When you book someone with that tag for a service on your rate card, the price is worked out for you and says why. Locked-in clients don't show under "On an old rate".

Getting to know them

Each client's page has a card for what's worth remembering about the person: the languages they speak and whether a translator is needed, cultural practices to respect, what's good to talk about and what to avoid. A client can be marked as passed away: their history stays, and the reminders stop.

After a finished booking, How did it go? asks a few quick questions (how it went, would you see them again, what you talked about). The answers are kept on the client's page.

Client base

Contacts → Client base shows who your clients are as simple bars: age, how they found you, line of work, languages, how often they've booked and what they've spent. It's built from what you've noted on their profiles, as counts only.

Drivers and rides

A driver is a contact you work with (not a client), and each keeps their own rates: per trip, hour, mile, km or day. Ride on a booking picks a driver and a rate and logs it as a travel expense that day, so it counts in the tour's budget.

Cancellations and no-shows

Settings → Deposits & cancellations sets your deposit percentage, what counts as a late cancellation, and the fees. Cancelling a booking asks who cancelled and suggests the fee from your policy.
