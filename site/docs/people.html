Clients & inquiries

Contacts

Contacts → Everyone holds clients, collaborators (another worker, a photographer, a driver: anyone you work with) and others. A name is optional, so a handle alone is fine. Each contact can have handles, a persona (the name they know you by), preferences, a birthday, screening status and references.

  • Screening: unscreened, pending, verified or declined, with notes on what you checked.
  • Do not see: tick it and Sworkr warns you if you try to book that person. A private bad-date list can be imported under Account.
  • How they found you: an ad site, an account, a referral. This is what makes Money → Compare → By how they found you work: income against what you spent on each.
  • Tags group people, and can change their price (below).

Inquiries

Contacts → Inquiries tracks everyone who got in touch: new, replied, screening, deposit, booked, or lost (with why). Move each along as it happens. Below the list: how far inquiries get, where they drop out, and which sources send people who actually book. Make a contact turns an inquiry into a contact when they're worth keeping. Your saved text is one tap away for replies.

Gone quiet

Contacts → Gone quiet lists regulars you've seen at least twice who haven't booked for a while and have nothing booked ahead, biggest spenders first, with how often they used to book.

Prices by tag

Money → Rates → Prices by tag: give a tag a percentage off, an amount off, or a locked-in rate (the client keeps paying what they last paid for that service). When you book someone with that tag for a service on your rate card, the price is worked out for you and says why. Locked-in clients don't show under "On an old rate".

Cancellations and no-shows

Account → Deposits & cancellations sets your deposit percentage, what counts as a late cancellation, and the fees. Cancelling a booking asks who cancelled and suggests the fee from your policy.
