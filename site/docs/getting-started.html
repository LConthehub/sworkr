Get started

Sworkr runs on your own computer. There is no account with us and nothing is sent to us. Pick one way to run it.

Windows app (easiest)

  1. Download Sworkr-windows.zip from the home page and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it, like Documents\Sworkr.
  2. Double-click Sworkr. Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the app is new: choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Your browser opens Sworkr. A small icon appears by the clock: click it to open Sworkr again, open your backups folder, or quit.

Your records are kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sworkr, your backups in Documents\Sworkr backups.

Docker (any computer, or a home server)

  1. Install Docker Desktop, open it, and wait for Engine running.
  2. Unzip sworkr-docker.zip. Windows: double-click start.bat. Mac: right-click start.command, choose Open. The first start takes a few minutes.
  3. Open http://localhost:8080.

Your first minutes

Create your account. That first account is yours and is the admin. Use a password of 12 or more characters and keep it somewhere safe. Then tick the kinds of work you do, and Sworkr sets itself up to match (expense categories, workplaces and their usual charges). You can change it any time under Account → My work.

A new copy has a few made-up clients so you can look around. Remove them under Account → Data & users → Example data.

Do these three things on day one

  1. Turn on backups. Account → Data & users → Backups. Choose a passphrase and tick automatic daily backups. Write the passphrase down: without it nobody can read a backup, and neither can you.
  2. Turn on two-step sign-in. Account → Security & privacy → Two-step sign-in, with an authenticator app. Save the recovery codes on paper.
  3. Encrypt your computer's disk (BitLocker on Windows, FileVault on a Mac) and use a locked user account.

Updating

Windows app: quit it, unzip the new version over the old folder, start it. Docker: stop it, unzip over the old folder (keep backups), start it. Your records are upgraded by themselves, and a copy is saved first.
