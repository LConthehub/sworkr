Privacy & backups

What's stored, and where

Everything is in one database file on your computer: clients, bookings, money, plans, settings. Sworkr doesn't send it anywhere. The only things it talks to are services you connect: your own email, a Nextcloud calendar or content folder, ntfy if you turn on notifications, SimpleFIN if you connect your bank, Bluesky, Mastodon or Reddit if you connect them for posting, and an AI model if you turn on the assistant (one on your own computer unless you allow a cloud one; it only gets totals, never names or notes).

How it's locked

  • The database file is encrypted (AES-256). A copy of the file on its own can't be read.
  • The key is kept apart from it. In the Windows app, it's in Windows Credential Manager, so it opens only for the person signed in to the computer. In Docker, it's in its own volume, sworkr_sworkr_keys.
  • Saved passwords (email, calendar, content folder, ntfy token) are sealed with a second key.
  • Sign-in: passkeys (face or fingerprint), two-step with an authenticator app, recovery codes, and a rule that can refuse passwords away from home.

What this does not do: protect against someone who can use your computer while you're signed in. Disk encryption, a locked computer account and discreet mode matter too.

Discreet mode

Settings → Security & privacy → Discreet mode gives the browser tab a plain name like "Notes" and adds a panic switch: press Esc three times, or tap Hide at the top on a phone, to hide Sworkr and sign out.

Backups

Backups are encrypted with your passphrase, not with the computer's key, so they open on any computer. That is how you get your records back if this computer dies.

  • Turn them on under Settings → Data & users → Backups. Automatic daily backups land in your backups folder.
  • Keep a copy off this computer: on a USB stick, or under Copies off this computer if you have a Nextcloud, a Synology NAS or any other WebDAV storage. Your own storage at home is the most private place for it.
  • Sworkr checks a backup opens about once a month and tells you if one doesn't.
  • To restore, use Restore a backup on the same page and enter the passphrase.

If the key is lost

Without the key, the database can't be opened. Never delete sworkr_sworkr_keys (Docker) or the Sworkr entries in Credential Manager. If it happens anyway, install Sworkr fresh and restore your latest backup with its passphrase.

Your data, your call

Settings → Data & users can download everything Sworkr holds about you, or erase everything. Erasing asks for your password, for you to type ERASE, then gives you 10 seconds to stop it. It can't reach backup files you've already saved elsewhere: delete those yourself.
