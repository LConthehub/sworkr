Health & safety

Health tests on your own schedule

Testing rules differ from place to place, so nothing is fixed. Settings → Health → Health tests: each test has a name, how many days apart it's due, and what it usually costs. Everyone starts with weekly, monthly and yearly, and you can rename them, change how often, add others (like "Quarterly bloodwork" every 90 days) or switch off what you don't use. Sworkr works out when the next one is due from the last, and shows it on the dashboard before then. A test's cost fills in when you add it to the calendar and is logged as an expense.

Documents that expire

Settings → My work → Licenses & documents: a license, work card, ID, passport, health card, a platform's ID check, insurance. Each gets a reminder before it runs out, and shows on the calendar. Sworkr keeps names and dates only: there's no field for ID or license numbers, on purpose.

Supplies

Settings → Health → Supplies is a short stock list. Tap minus when you use one; what's running low shows on your dashboard.

Safety check-in

Settings → Safety → Safety check-in sets up a trusted contact. Tick Safety check-in on a booking; if you haven't checked in afterwards (within the grace time you set), your contact is emailed what you chose to share. This needs email set up under Connections, or a push address: your contact installs the free ntfy app and subscribes to a long random name you agree on. A push arrives in seconds, where email can take minutes.

The safety timer on the dashboard uses the same contact for anything that isn't a booking. If the alert already went out when you tap I'm safe, your contact gets an all-clear.

Incident log and privacy check-up

The incident log is under Settings → Safety, the privacy check-up under Settings → Security & privacy. The incident log is for writing down what happened the same day: when, what, who, and what you kept. It's never sent anywhere. The privacy check-up is a checklist for keeping work and private life apart.

Period tracker, birth control and regular meds

Optional, off until you turn them on. Injections can rotate sites and step up doses on a schedule. Dates show on the calendar, and medication marked discreet shows only as "Health".

Wellness log

Also optional (Settings → Health → Wellness log): a daily check-in on the dashboard for sleep, water, how well you ate and your energy. After a few weeks it shows how your earnings compare on better and worse days. Sleep and water can be filled in from an iPhone's Health app export.

Holidays

Settings → My work → Holidays puts your country's public holidays on the calendar. They're worked out on your own computer: no calendar is downloaded from anyone.
