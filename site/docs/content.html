Content & planning

The calendar plans everything

Calendar shows bookings, tours, time off and health tests, and also what you've planned: posts to put up, shoots and the day each ad runs out. Tick or untick Planning at the bottom to show only what you want. + New adds a booking, a post or a shoot. Click a planned item to open it. Posts and Shoots at the top hold the full lists.

Posts

Write the caption, pick the platform and time. For Bluesky, Mastodon and Reddit, connect your account under Content → Social and Sworkr can post it now or by itself at its time, with a content label and a description of each picture (alt text). Elsewhere, copy the caption when it's due, post it yourself, then mark it posted with the link and how many inquiries it brought. Content → Social also shows likes, reposts and replies, fetched or imported from a platform's CSV export.

  • One post, many platforms. Also post it on makes a row per platform, each with its own caption and time.
  • Your platforms (Content → Promote → Platforms & saved text): each account or channel with its rules and how often you may post there. Planning a post too soon after the last one warns you.
  • Saved text: links, hashtags, your booking line: one tap to add to a caption.

Shoots

Calendar → Shoots plans a content shoot in one place: the date, the place and what to know about it, the people (photographer, co-star, makeup) and what you agreed about the content, goals, shot list, wardrobe, what to prepare, a budget and the marketing plan. It warns you about what's missing as the day gets close, and nobody on camera is "done" until their ID is checked and their release signed. Pay a budget line or a fee and it's logged as an expense against the shoot. Start a campaign for it carries the shoot into your promotion.

Promote

Content → Promote holds campaigns (a push with a goal and dates, such as a tour announcement or a set launch, with its posts together) and ads (each listing's site, end date and cost; Renewed moves the date and logs the cost). For a tour campaign, Suggest tour posts adds the usual run of ideas: a week before, the day before, arrival, last day.

Library

Content → Library shows the folder you already keep your photos and videos in, from Nextcloud, a Synology or other WebDAV server, or a folder on your computer. Each subfolder is a set. Sworkr only reads it, never copies or changes anything, and shows where each file has already been posted. Connect it under Settings → Connections → Content library.

Customs

Content → Customs follows made-to-order content from request to delivery: who asked, what they want, the price, what's paid, when it's due. Got paid adds the payment to your income as a sale (minus the platform's fee) and says how it was paid. Sworkr flags customs being made before they're fully paid for, and late ones. Your menu keeps your usual prices and copies as text.
