Nobody holds your data but you

Sworkr isn't a website you sign up to. It's a program that runs on your own computer, and your records never leave it. There's no company server holding a list of workers and their clients, so there's nothing to leak, sell, subpoena or shut down.

  • Encrypted. Your records are stored encrypted, and the key sits in your computer's own keychain.
  • Backups only you can open. They're encrypted with your passphrase, and they restore on any computer.
  • Passkeys and two-step sign-in. Use your face or fingerprint, with no password to steal.
  • Discreet mode. A plain name in the browser tab, and a panic key that hides everything.
  • On your phone, privately. Your phone connects to your own computer. Nothing is opened to the internet.
  • No tracking. Not in the app, and not on this site.

Fair questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for workers. There's nothing to subscribe to, because there's no service to run: it's software on your own computer. Use it on your own or with up to five workers sharing one setup, for free. Agencies, houses, clubs and platforms need a license (the terms).

Can you see my clients?

No. We don't have them. Sworkr never sends your records anywhere. The only things it connects to are the ones you set up yourself, like your own email or calendar.

What if my computer dies?

Turn on backups on day one. They're encrypted with a passphrase you choose, and they restore on any other computer. Keep a copy somewhere other than that computer too, like a USB stick or your own cloud.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Sworkr walks you through linking your phone to your computer privately, then you add it to your home screen like an app. Your computer needs to be on.

Why isn't it in the App Store?

Because your records belong on a device you control, not on a platform that can remove the app or change its rules.
