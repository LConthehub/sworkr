Keep Sworkr free for workers.

Sworkr is made by a former s worker, for the people still doing the work. There's no company, no ads, and nothing sold. If it makes your work easier, you can help keep it going.

Give what fits

$5

A coffee. Every bit keeps the lights on.

$25

Pays for a month of the website and the demo.

$100

Pays for testing on real phones and the time to fix what's found.

Any amount

Once, or every month. Whatever you can spare is plenty.

Two ways to give

Card

Through GitHub Sponsors, once or monthly. GitHub shows your GitHub name to the project unless you choose to give privately.

Give by card

Crypto

Bitcoin (and Lightning) through Sworkr's own payment page. No company in the middle, and nothing that can be closed down.

Give with crypto

Card payment companies sometimes close accounts tied to adult work, even for software. That's why there are two ways: if one stops, the other still works.

Other ways to help

  • Tell a friend who could use it.
  • Try the demo and say what's confusing or missing.
  • Report a problem on GitHub.
